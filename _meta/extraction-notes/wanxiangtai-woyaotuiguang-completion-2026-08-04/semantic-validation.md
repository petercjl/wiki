# Semantic validation

## Validation questions

- Can a user navigate from the root to all ten official modules? Yes.
- Does each module explain its business problem instead of mirroring source titles? Yes.
- Are historical campaigns and dynamic rules separated from stable methods? Yes.
- Are unsupported nodes accounted for without fabricated content? Yes, 30 nodes.
- Are acquisition, targeting, product, content, campaign, CRM, compliance and diagnosis represented? Yes.
- Are Activity Assistant and Case Cube preserved as independent official top-level modules? Yes.
- Can source facts be traced to raw packages? Yes, via frontmatter sources and module inventories.

## Known limitations

- Current UI, eligibility and pricing require live verification before execution.
- Some directory/form nodes could not be represented as document bodies.
- Not every screenshot is embedded in a formal page; all remain retrievable from raw manifests.
