## Raw-Only With Reason

- Codec, file size and other acquisition metadata: provenance rather than durable filmmaking method.
- Repeated instructor talking-head frames: duplicate visual content.
- Black transition frames: editorial separators with no additional knowledge.
- Full film dialogue after 01:26: ASR is demonstrably unreliable and the first chapter does not supply a verified script on-screen.

## Versioned or Intentionally Unresolved

- Exact dialogue is preserved only where present in verified Blog prompts or Academy text; unreliable showcase-video ASR remains excluded.
- Whether “4K” means native generation, platform upscale or delivery resolution: this chapter only makes the source claim.
- Exact causal strength of the single-face rule across current/future models: treat as Higgsfield/Seedance working practice, not universal model law.
- Academy figure files: 48 URLs and captions are indexed, but local CDN downloads failed TLS negotiation; existing Blog and scene-pack images provide the evidence used by formal pages.
- Lesson 2 full-script PDF direct endpoint: not exposed in captured DOM; page resource label is archived.
- Current platform behavior of versioned models and UI paths; preserve source date and retest before execution.

## No Silent Loss Check

- All instructor claims before the film have source-inventory and coverage rows.
- Every sampled frame has an image-inventory disposition.
- Music/action ASR hallucinations are preserved and explicitly excluded rather than silently cleaned.
- The visual film is represented by production blocks and continuity relations rather than reduced to “demo footage.”
- Every Blog heading, prompt panel, informative image and download block has an inventory disposition.
- Exact prompts remain verbatim in raw Markdown; later formal pages may abstract them only when the original remains directly recoverable.
- Every Academy lesson from 2 through 10 has a dedicated coverage row and formal target.
