# THE CONFRONTATION - RISING ACTION

## THE CONFRONTATION - RISING ACTION

In a story’s middle, we need conflict and action to heat up. In the middle, you’re dramatizing the protagonist’s confrontation with problems and how they affect her. Without obstacles, complications and crises for the characters, a story stalls. I’ve defined these elements in Chapter 4 and can’t stress enough their importance. If your story is a lot of scenes with your protagonist going places, doing things, talking with people, but no conflict develops, your script won’t be very exciting. When you use the conflict effectively, you’re able to build suspense and generate surprise to enhance your plot and better excite your audience. These tools will help you strengthen the middle.
