No matter what editing system you are using, you will 
always be confronting astronomical numbers of dif-

So, paradoxically, the hidden advantage of edit- 
ing sprocketed film was that the weight and volume 
of it encouraged the editor to take things seriously 
and to plan ahead before jumping in-to develop cer- 
tain strategies and defensive mechanisms. For me, this 
took the form of my "reverse storyboard" system of 
images and my database system of notation. All of us 
who have grown up as editors of sprocketed film were 
privileged to develop our own strategies for dealing 
with our own Amazons, and many of these strategies 
should not now be thoughtlessly discarded, but rather 
adapted to the digital age that is upon us.
