Preface 
Igor Stravinsky loved expressing himself and wrote a 
good deal on interpretation. As he bore a volcano 
within him, he urged restraint. Those without even 
the vestige of a volcano within them nodded in 
agreement, raised their baton, and observed restraint, 
while Stravinsky himself conducted his own Apollon 
Musagete as if it were Tchaikovsky. We who had 
read him listened and were astonished.
